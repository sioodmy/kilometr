Kilometr plans public transport journeys on your phone. Download timetables to find connections and check departures without internet. Download the offline map separately in the app.

• Routes with transfers, walking times and transport filters.
• Stop departures and saved places.
• Journey tracking, local notifications and departure reminders.
• Vehicle positions and service news when online.
• Back up settings and saved places to a file.

No ads or account. Available in Polish, English, German and Ukrainian.
